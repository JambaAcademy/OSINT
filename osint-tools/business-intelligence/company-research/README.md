# Company Research

## Overview

This section covers general-purpose platforms for researching a company's structure, history, funding, personnel, and public communications. See `company-research-tracker.csv` in this folder for a ready-to-use tracking template.

---

## Company Registry and Profile Platforms

| Tool | Description | Best For | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| OpenCorporates | Aggregates official company registry data from many jurisdictions (also listed in `search-and-discovery/government-databases/`) | Cross-jurisdiction company registration lookup | Freemium |
| Crunchbase | Database of companies, funding rounds, executives, and investors, focused on startups and venture-backed companies | Startup and venture funding research | Freemium/Paid |
| PitchBook | Comprehensive private market data platform covering companies, deals, and investors | In-depth private equity and venture capital research | Paid, enterprise |
| ZoomInfo | Business contact and company data platform | Combining company profiles with contact discovery for sales/business development | Paid |
| Bloomberg company profiles | Financial data platform with detailed company profiles for public and many private companies | Institutional-grade company and market data | Paid, enterprise |

## Corporate Communications and Public Filings

| Resource | Description | Best For | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Company investor relations pages | Public companies maintain dedicated investor relations sections with filings, presentations, and earnings call materials | Primary-source company financial communications | Free |
| Earnings call transcript services | Various services and news aggregators publish or summarize public company earnings call transcripts | Understanding management's own characterization of company performance and strategy | Freemium/Paid |
| Company press release archives (e.g., PR Newswire, Business Wire) | Wire services that distribute and archive corporate press releases | Tracking a company's public announcements over time | Free (browsing); paid for distribution services |

## Employee and Organizational Insight

| Resource | Description | Best For | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn company pages | Official company page plus aggregated employee headcount and role data | Estimating headcount, department structure, and hiring trends | Free (enhanced with paid tiers) |
| Glassdoor / Indeed company reviews | Employee-submitted reviews and salary information | Understanding company culture and compensation as reported by current/former employees (self-reported, unverified) | Free |
| Job posting aggregators | Company job postings often reveal technology stack, growth areas, and organizational structure | Inferring company priorities and technical direction | Free |

## Building a Company Profile

1. Establish the legal entity structure using `search-and-discovery/government-databases/` (registry filings, parent/subsidiary relationships).
2. Gather financial context using `financial-analysis/` (public filings if applicable).
3. Review public communications (press releases, investor relations, earnings calls) for the company's own narrative.
4. Cross-check employee-reported insight (Glassdoor, LinkedIn) against the official narrative for consistency.
5. Log findings in `company-research-tracker.csv` (this folder) as you go.

---

## Usage Notes

- Startup/venture-focused databases (Crunchbase, PitchBook) have significantly better coverage of well-funded, venture-backed companies than of small private businesses, government contractors, or companies outside major startup ecosystems; do not assume absence from these databases indicates a company does not exist or is not significant.
- Employee review platforms are self-reported and can be affected by both disgruntled-employee bias and company-incentivized positive reviews; read for pattern and volume rather than any single review.

---

## Legal and Ethical Notes

- Company research techniques in this section rely on publicly available registry, communications, and review platform data.
- Where company research surfaces named individuals (executives, officers), apply the elevated standard in `people-investigation/README.md` if further investigation into that specific individual is warranted.
