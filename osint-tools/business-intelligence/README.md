# Business Intelligence

## Overview

This category covers tools and resources for researching companies and organizations: general company research, financial statement analysis, regulatory and compliance monitoring, and competitive intelligence. Unlike `people-investigation/`, the subject matter here is primarily organizational rather than personal, though the same proportionality and legal-basis principles apply whenever research touches on named individuals within an organization (executives, officers, beneficial owners).

Alongside tool directories, this category includes several ready-to-use working files: a company research tracker, an SEC EDGAR filing lookup script, a sanctions list screening script, and a competitive intelligence tracker.

---

## When to Use This Category

- Due diligence on a business partner, vendor, acquisition target, or investment.
- Understanding a competitor's market position, product strategy, or public communications.
- Verifying a company's regulatory standing, financial health, or beneficial ownership structure.
- Building a company profile as background for a broader investigation involving that organization.
