# Legal and Ethics

## Overview

This section provides educational, narrative background on the legal and ethical dimensions of OSINT practice, complementing the operational `osint-templates/operational-planning/legal-compliance-checklist.md`, which is designed as an in-the-moment working checklist rather than explanatory reading. See `international-legal-frameworks-guide.md` for a comparative overview of how major jurisdictions approach data protection and OSINT-relevant law.

## How to Use This Section Alongside the Operational Checklist

- Read this section's guides when you want to understand *why* a checklist item exists and how frameworks differ across jurisdictions.
- Use `osint-templates/operational-planning/legal-compliance-checklist.md` during an actual investigation as your working, in-the-moment reference.
- Neither this section nor the checklist constitutes legal advice; consult qualified legal counsel for guidance on a specific investigation, especially one involving multiple jurisdictions or higher legal risk (see `osint-templates/operational-planning/risk-assessment-matrix.md`).
