## 2. Risk Rating Methodology

### 2.1 Likelihood Scale

| Rating | Description |
|---|---|
| Low | Unlikely to occur under normal investigation conditions |
| Medium | Plausible under foreseeable circumstances |
| High | Likely or expected given the nature of this specific investigation |

### 2.2 Impact Scale

| Rating | Description |
|---|---|
| Low | Minor inconvenience; no lasting effect on investigation, organization, or subject |
| Medium | Moderate disruption, remediable with effort; possible limited legal or reputational exposure |
| High | Severe consequence; potential legal liability, significant harm to a subject, or major reputational damage |

### 2.3 Overall Risk Rating Grid

| Likelihood \ Impact | Low | Medium | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Low** | Low | Low | Medium |
| **Medium** | Low | Medium | High |
| **High** | Medium | High | High |
