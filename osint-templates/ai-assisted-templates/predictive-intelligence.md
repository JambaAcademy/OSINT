# Predictive Intelligence Report

## Purpose Statement

This template documents forward-looking assessments produced using trend analysis, statistical forecasting, or AI-assisted predictive modeling applied to OSINT-derived data. Predictive intelligence is inherently probabilistic and uncertain; this template is structured to ensure forecasts are presented with explicit confidence levels, stated assumptions, and a clear distinction between historical fact and forward-looking judgment, consistent with established structured analytic technique practice.
