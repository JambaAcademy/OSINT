# Court-Ready OSINT Report

## Purpose Statement

This template is designed for OSINT findings intended for submission in, or preparation to support, a legal proceeding (civil litigation, family law matter, regulatory enforcement action, or similar). It emphasizes the formality, sourcing rigor, and evidentiary discipline that legal proceedings typically require. This template does not constitute legal advice; consult retaining counsel regarding the specific evidentiary rules and formatting requirements of the relevant court or tribunal.
