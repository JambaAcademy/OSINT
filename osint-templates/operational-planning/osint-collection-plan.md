# OSINT Collection Plan

## Purpose Statement

This document defines the systematic collection strategy for an open-source intelligence investigation prior to active collection activity beginning. Completing this plan before collection starts helps ensure the investigation stays within legal and ethical bounds, remains focused on stated objectives, and produces defensible, well-sourced findings.

## 6. Legal and Ethical Review

### 6.1 Pre-Collection Checklist

- [ ] Investigation objective is legitimate and proportionate to the information being sought
- [ ] Only publicly accessible or lawfully obtained information will be collected
- [ ] Planned sources and techniques comply with the terms of service of each platform
- [ ] No planned technique involves unauthorized access, deception to obtain non-public information, or circumvention of access controls
- [ ] Data minimization has been considered: only information relevant to the stated objective will be collected
- [ ] Applicable privacy law obligations (see Section 1.2) have been reviewed
