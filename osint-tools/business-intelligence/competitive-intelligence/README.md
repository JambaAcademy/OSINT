# Competitive Intelligence

## Overview

This section covers tools for tracking a competitor's market position, web traffic, advertising activity, and public communications. See `competitive-intelligence-tracker.xlsx` in this folder for a ready-to-use tracking workbook. All techniques in this section rely on publicly observable information and do not involve any unauthorized access to a competitor's systems or non-public data.

---

## Web Traffic and Digital Presence Analysis

| Tool | Description | Best For | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| SimilarWeb | Estimates website traffic volume, sources, and audience demographics | Comparing relative digital presence and traffic trends across competitors | Freemium/Paid |
| SEMrush | SEO and digital marketing intelligence platform, including competitor keyword and backlink analysis | Understanding a competitor's search and content strategy | Freemium/Paid |
| Ahrefs | SEO analysis platform with strong backlink and keyword ranking data | Competitive content and search visibility analysis | Paid |
| BuiltWith (also listed in `technical-reconnaissance/infrastructure-mapping/`) | Technology stack profiler | Understanding a competitor's technology and vendor choices | Freemium/Paid |

## Advertising and Marketing Intelligence

| Tool | Description | Best For | Cost |

| Meta Ad Library (also listed in `social-media-intelligence/platform-specific-tools/`) | Public archive of active and historical Meta platform ads | Tracking a competitor's social advertising messaging and targeting | Free |

## Product and Pricing Intelligence

| Tool/Technique | Description | Best For | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct product page monitoring | Manually or automatically tracking a competitor's public pricing and product pages over time | Tracking pricing and product changes | Free (manual); paid tools available for automated tracking |
| Change-tracking services | Various services notify users when a specified public web page changes | Passive monitoring of a competitor's public pages without manual re-checking | Freemium/Paid |
| App store listing monitoring | Public app store listings reveal update frequency, feature changelogs, and user review sentiment | Tracking a competitor's product development pace and user reception | Free |

## Usage Notes

- Third-party traffic estimation tools (SimilarWeb, SEMrush) provide modeled estimates, not ground-truth data directly from the competitor; treat reported figures as directionally useful for trend and relative comparison rather than precise absolute numbers.
- Ad transparency libraries only capture ads run through that specific platform's ad system; a competitor's broader marketing activity (television, print, direct mail, other ad networks) will not appear there.

---

## Legal and Ethical Notes

- All tools and techniques in this section rely on publicly observable information (public websites, public ad transparency archives, public job postings) and do not involve accessing a competitor's internal systems, non-public pricing, or confidential business information.
- Competitive intelligence gathering should remain within the bounds of fair competition law in your jurisdiction; consult legal counsel regarding any activity that could be construed as unfair competition, trade secret misappropriation, or improper solicitation of a competitor's employees for information.
