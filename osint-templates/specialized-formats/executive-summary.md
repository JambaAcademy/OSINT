# Executive Summary Template

## Purpose Statement

This template provides a standardized structure for condensing a full OSINT investigation report into a concise executive summary suitable for senior leadership, board members, or other stakeholders who require the key findings and recommendations without the full technical detail. An executive summary should typically be one to two pages regardless of the length of the underlying full report.

## Formatting Guidance for This Template

- Keep total length to one to two pages; if it runs longer, move detail to the full report and link to it.
- Write for a reader with no prior context on the investigation's technical detail.
- Avoid jargon and acronyms without expansion on first use.
- Lead with the conclusion, not the methodology; methodology belongs in the full report.
- Use active voice and specific, concrete language rather than hedged generalities.
- Ensure every claim in this summary is traceable to a specific section of the full report for any reader who wants to verify it.
